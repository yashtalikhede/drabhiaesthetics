Meet the Doctor

Dr. Abhisek Yadav

Dr. Abhisek Yadav founded Dr. Abhi Aesthetics on a simple frustration. A patient with adult acne is sent to one clinic, the same patient's hair fall to another, and their weight to a third, and nobody joins the dots.

Hormonal patterns surface as breakouts. Nutritional gaps surface as thinning hair. Metabolic problems surface as weight that will not move. Read separately, each gets a partial answer. Read together, they usually point to one cause.

That is the whole argument for this clinic. Skin, hair and fitness sit with one doctor, on one medical record, so the pattern is visible.

Consultations are unhurried and diagnosis-led. You will be told what is actually driving the problem, what can realistically improve and, just as plainly, what cannot.
