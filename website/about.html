Introduction

Dr. Abhisek Yadav founded Dr. Abhi Aesthetics to treat skin, hair and physical health as one connected system. Too often a patient is sent to one clinic for acne, another for hair fall and a third for weight — with nobody joining the dots between them.

At this clinic, those three threads sit with one doctor. A hormonal pattern showing up as adult acne, a nutritional gap showing up as hair thinning, a metabolic issue showing up as stubborn weight — these are read together, not in isolation.

Consultations are unhurried and diagnosis-led. You will be told what is actually causing the problem, what can realistically be improved, and what cannot.
