Category Two

Hair & Trichology

Diagnosis-led hair and scalp care, from hair fall investigation through to restoration planning.

PRP Therapy for Hair Loss Consultation required

Platelet-rich plasma prepared from your own blood sample and delivered into the scalp to support existing follicles. Usually planned as a course of sessions, with response reviewed at set intervals.

Duration: [duration TBC]

Hair Fall Diagnosis Consultation required

A structured investigation into why hair is shedding or thinning — covering scalp examination, pattern assessment, and where indicated, blood work for nutritional, thyroid or hormonal causes. This is the recommended starting point for any hair concern.

Duration: [duration TBC]

Dandruff & Scalp Care Consultation required

Treatment for flaking, itching, seborrhoeic dermatitis and other scalp conditions. Persistent dandruff is frequently a driver of hair fall, so the scalp is treated as part of the wider hair plan rather than separately.

Duration: [duration TBC]

Mesotherapy Consultation required

Micro-delivery of a targeted nutrient and medication blend into the scalp to support follicle health. Often combined with other treatments as part of a staged hair programme.

Duration: [duration TBC]

Laser Hair Reduction Consultation required

Laser treatment to reduce unwanted hair growth on the face or body. Results build across a planned series of sessions, and the number required varies with hair colour, thickness and the area being treated.

Duration: [duration TBC]

Hair Transplant Consultation Consultation required

An honest assessment of whether a transplant is appropriate for you — covering donor area quality, graft estimates, expected density and realistic outcomes. Many patients are advised to stabilise ongoing hair loss medically before considering surgery.

Hair Spa Consultation required

A conditioning and scalp-nourishing treatment for dryness, dullness and product build-up. Supportive care rather than a medical treatment, and best used alongside a clinical plan where hair fall is also a concern.
