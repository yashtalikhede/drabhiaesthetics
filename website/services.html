Dermatology

Skin & Dermatology

Medical and cosmetic dermatology for active conditions, texture, tone and ageing concerns.

Acne Treatment Consultation required

Medical management of active acne, from comedonal breakouts to inflammatory and hormonal patterns. Treatment begins by identifying the trigger — hormonal, bacterial, dietary or product-related — and builds a topical, oral or in-clinic plan from there.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Acne Scar Reduction Consultation required

Resurfacing for post-acne scarring and uneven texture. The approach depends on scar type — boxcar, rolling or icepick — and typically runs over a planned series of sessions rather than a single visit.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Pigmentation Treatment Consultation required

Targeted care for melasma, sun-induced pigmentation and post-inflammatory marks. Includes assessing the depth of pigment, which determines how much improvement is realistically achievable and over what timeframe.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Skin Brightening Consultation required

Treatments aimed at a more even tone and improved clarity. Focused on correcting dullness and patchiness rather than altering your natural complexion.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Anti-Ageing Treatments Consultation required

Management of fine lines, loss of firmness and age-related volume change. Plans are built to keep expression natural, with a clear explanation of what each option can and cannot change.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Chemical Peels Consultation required

Controlled exfoliation to improve texture, congestion, pigmentation and dullness. Peel strength and formulation are matched to your skin type and the downtime you can accommodate.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Mole & Wart Removal Consultation required

Minor in-clinic removal of moles, warts, skin tags and similar lesions. Every lesion is clinically assessed first; anything suspicious is referred for investigation before any cosmetic removal.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Laser Skin Treatment Consultation required

Laser-based resurfacing and toning for texture, pigmentation and overall skin quality. Suitability depends on your skin type and recent sun exposure, both assessed beforehand.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Under Eye Treatment Consultation required

Care for dark circles, hollowing and crepey texture. The cause matters here — pigment, shadow from volume loss and vascular darkness each respond to different treatments, so the consultation separates them first.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Trichology

Hair & Trichology

Diagnosis-led hair and scalp care, from hair fall investigation through to restoration planning.

PRP Therapy for Hair Loss Consultation required

Platelet-rich plasma prepared from your own blood sample and delivered into the scalp to support existing follicles. Usually planned as a course of sessions, with response reviewed at set intervals.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Hair Fall Diagnosis Consultation required

A structured investigation into why hair is shedding or thinning — scalp examination, pattern assessment and, where indicated, blood work for nutritional, thyroid or hormonal causes. The recommended starting point for any hair concern.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Dandruff & Scalp Care Consultation required

Treatment for flaking, itching, seborrhoeic dermatitis and other scalp conditions. Persistent dandruff frequently drives hair fall, so the scalp is treated as part of the wider hair plan.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Mesotherapy Consultation required

Micro-delivery of a targeted nutrient and medication blend into the scalp to support follicle health. Often combined with other treatments as part of a staged hair programme.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Laser Hair Reduction Consultation required

Laser treatment to reduce unwanted hair growth on the face or body. Results build across a planned series of sessions; the number required varies with hair colour, thickness and the area treated.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Hair Transplant Consultation Consultation required

An honest assessment of whether a transplant suits you — donor area quality, graft estimates, expected density and realistic outcomes. Many patients are advised to stabilise ongoing loss medically before considering surgery.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Hair Spa Consultation required

A conditioning and scalp-nourishing treatment for dryness, dullness and product build-up. Supportive care rather than a medical treatment, best used alongside a clinical plan where hair fall is also a concern.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Fitness Medicine

Fitness & Wellness

Medically supervised fitness and nutrition, built around measurement rather than guesswork.

Body Composition Analysis Consultation required

Measurement of fat mass, lean muscle, water balance and distribution — the baseline that makes progress measurable. Far more useful than bodyweight alone, and repeated periodically to track real change.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Weight Management Consultation required

A medically supervised programme for sustainable weight loss or gain, accounting for metabolic, thyroid and hormonal factors. Built for gradual, maintainable change rather than crash protocols.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Diet & Nutrition Planning Consultation required

A personalised plan based on your routine, food preferences, regional cuisine and any medical conditions. Designed to fit the way you actually eat, which is what makes a plan last.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Muscle Building Consultation required

Structured guidance on training load, protein intake and recovery for lean mass gain. Progress is tracked against composition measurements rather than appearance alone.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Lifestyle Consultation Consultation required

A review of sleep, stress, activity and daily habits — the factors that quietly drive skin, hair and weight outcomes. Frequently the missing piece when results have plateaued.

Session length confirmed at your consultation

Online Fitness Consultation Consultation required

Remote consultation and follow-up for patients outside Pune or unable to attend in person. Suitable for plan reviews and progress check-ins; the initial assessment is better done at the clinic where measurement is possible.

Session length confirmed at your consultation
